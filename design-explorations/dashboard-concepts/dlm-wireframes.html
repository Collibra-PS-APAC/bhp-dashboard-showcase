DLM role dashboards - low-fidelity wireframes

Three role-specific dashboards for the Data Lifecycle Management workflow, one per persona: Technical Application Owner (TAO), Business Application Owner (BAO), and Legal Officer. Each blends an estate / compliance overview (top) with that role's own action queue (below), the "both in one" layout. Content follows the BHP DLM RACI (TAO enforces technically, BAO approves for the business, Legal owns holds and disposal clearance). Numbers are live from bhp-test; per-user counts are illustrative until the queue is filtered by candidate. Greyscale and structure only - the BHP brand pass comes next.
BHP  Collibra Data Intelligence Platform  ·  DLM — Technical Application Owner

Technical Application Owner. Owns technical enforcement of lifecycle rules: configure retention, run archival, execute disposal, apply and clear holds in the system, capture audit evidence (RACI processes P1-P5, mostly Responsible). The dashboard leads with what needs enforcing now - retention to apply, disposals past due, holds to implement.
